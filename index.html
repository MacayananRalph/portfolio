My Journey

Career journey from 2020 to 2026.

2020

Started My Creative Journey

My entry into creative media began with a spark of curiosity about video editing.

2021

Collaborations

I started joining editing community to expand my network, gain ideas and learn about their experiences.

2022

Graphic Design

I was introduced to graphic design through my friends in the communities I joined in, which inspired me to start experimenting with visual concepts and crafting my very first designs.

2023

Learning & Growing

I continued refining my skills and experimenting with various editing styles, a natural curiosity led me straight into the world of motion graphics.

2024

UI Design

During my academic studies, I was introduced to UI/UX Design—a subject that immediately captured my interest. Intrigued by the intersection of user psychology and visual design, I began actively pursuing the discipline, dedicated to mastering the principles of user-centered design and interactive interfaces.

2025

Expanding My Work

Recognizing early on how fast design technology evolves, I decided to adapt by continuously exploring new creative tools and testing their workflows.

2026

Where I Am Now

To elevate my creative work to the next level, I recently transitioned to desktop editing using Adobe After Effects, allowing me to push the boundaries of my previous projects and bring more depth to my craft.
